Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026 • Version: 2.4.0 • Entity: New Smart Agent (newsmartagent.com)

1. Introduction

Welcome to New Smart Agent ("NSA", "we", "us", or "our"), accessible at https://newsmartagent.com. We provide an AI-powered omnichannel automation SaaS platform designed to streamline customer communications across Meta platforms (Facebook Messenger, Instagram DM, Page comments) and connected third-party channels.

This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, usage, storage, and safeguarding of information when you connect your social media pages, utilize our automation engine, or register an account with us. By using New Smart Agent, you acknowledge and agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

Meta Platform & Graph API Compliance

2. Facebook & Meta Data Collection and Usage

When you connect your Facebook Page or Instagram Business account to New Smart Agent, our platform interacts with Meta's Graph API strictly within the bounds of the permissions you explicitly grant during the OAuth authorization process.

A. Data Points We Access

  • Page Identifiers & Tokens: Facebook Page ID, Page name, and secure Page Access Tokens required to invoke Graph API endpoints on your behalf.
  • Customer Messages & Metadata: Inbound messages sent to your Facebook Page inbox, message sender scoped IDs (PSID), timestamps, and conversation threads.
  • Comment Interactions: Public comments and replies made on your Facebook Page posts and connected advertisements.
  • Public Profile Details: Public sender name and profile picture URL provided by Meta via webhook events solely to personalize replies in customer support threads.

B. Purpose of Processing

  • Executing real-time automated AI responses to customer queries in Messenger.
  • Automatically moderating and replying to comments on Facebook posts.
  • Facilitating seamless escalation and hand-off to live human support agents.
  • Tracking conversation resolution and customer engagement metrics.

C. Token Handling & Encryption

All Meta Page Access Tokens are stored with industry-standard cryptographic encryption (AES-256) inside our backend database. Tokens are never exposed to client browsers or unauthorized internal services. You may revoke these tokens at any time directly through your Facebook Page settings or through our dashboard.

3. Artificial Intelligence Data Processing

New Smart Agent utilizes state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs) to formulate accurate, contextually relevant replies to customer inquiries.

  • Zero Generalized Model Training: We never use customer message contents, private correspondence, or Meta user data to train, fine-tune, or improve generalized foundation AI models.
  • Contextual Processing Only: Inbound text is passed to processing pipelines via secure, zero-data-retention API agreements strictly to evaluate intent and output instant responses.
  • Business Isolation: Knowledge base documents and catalogs uploaded by a merchant are strictly partitioned in isolated tenant databases and never accessible to other accounts.

4. Data Retention and User Data Deletion Rights

We respect user privacy and adhere to the principle of data minimization:

  • Ephemeral Message Processing: Inbound message payloads from webhooks are held in cache only as long as necessary to formulate responses and update conversation logs.
  • Account Deletion: If you delete your New Smart Agent account or disconnect your Meta business page, all corresponding access tokens, cached threads, and credentials are permanently purged within 30 days.
  • Direct Deletion Callback: For detailed instructions on removing New Smart Agent from your Facebook profile and submitting a manual deletion request, visit our User Data Deletion Instructions Page.

5. Data Security and Infrastructure

We apply multi-layered security controls to protect your confidential business data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All HTTP traffic is protected by TLS 1.3 / SSL encryption through Cloudflare's security layer.
  • Access Isolation: Production databases are hosted behind private virtual networks with strict IP whitelisting and no direct public internet exposure.
  • Authentication: Session credentials utilize HttpOnly secure cookies and JWT tokens with short lifetimes to mitigate session hijacking.

6. Third-Party Sharing & Prohibition on Data Sale

New Smart Agent does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal information, customer communication data, or Meta platform data to third-party data brokers, advertising networks, or unauthorized third parties.

We disclose information solely to authorized cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., Cloudflare, cloud hosting nodes) strictly necessary to deliver the SaaS service.
