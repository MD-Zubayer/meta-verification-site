User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with the Meta Platform Developer Policy (§4.b) and global privacy regulations (GDPR & CCPA), New Smart Agent provides users with complete autonomy to revoke application access and request permanent deletion of all stored data.

Method 1: Disconnect via Facebook Account Settings (Instant)

Immediate token revocation through Meta interface

You can remove New Smart Agent and immediately revoke our access to your Facebook Pages by following these steps:

  1. Log in to your Facebook account and click on your profile picture in the top-right corner.
  2. Navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  3. In the left-hand menu, scroll down to the Security / Permissions section and select Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).
  4. Locate New Smart Agent in the list of active integrations.
  5. Click the Remove button next to New Smart Agent.
  6. In the confirmation popup, optionally check the box to delete all posts, videos, or events published on your behalf, and click Remove to confirm.
Result: Facebook immediately invalidates your Page Access Token. Our backend will receive a real-time deauthorization webhook and instantly halt all automated messages and actions for your pages.

Method 2: Request Complete Data Purge via Email

Full wipe of database records, logs, and cached messages

If you wish to have all stored records (such as your account profile, transaction history, message metadata, and cached interaction logs) permanently wiped from our production servers:

To: support@newsmartagent.com

Subject: User Data Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email or Page ID]

Body Requirements:

  • Registered account email address on New Smart Agent.
  • Facebook Page Name and Page ID (if applicable).
  • Explicit confirmation statement requesting permanent deletion.

Our engineering team will verify the request, execute the permanent erasure across our database and cache partitions within 7 to 30 calendar days, and issue an official Confirmation of Deletion email with a permanent verification hash.

What Data Is Permanently Purged?

Access Tokens & Credentials

All Page Access Tokens, webhook subscriptions, and encrypted API keys are deleted immediately.

Inbound Message Cache

All temporary conversation contexts and prompt memory buffers are wiped from Redis and PostgreSQL.

Lead & Contact Records

Customer profiles captured via social Messenger flows are anonymized or permanently deleted.

Merchant Account Profile

User account details, settings, and automation templates are permanently removed.
